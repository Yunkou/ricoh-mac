# v0.2.0

- The app now opens on two entries: Photos on the left and Devices on the right. Click either card to enter.
- Devices connects to a saved camera so you can browse, select, and import photos. After an import, you can open the photos you just brought in.
- Photos lets you browse photos already imported to this Mac, without connecting a camera. The app reads these files and does not change the originals.
- Open a photo to view it larger. Use the arrow keys or the buttons to move to the previous or next photo, and use shortcuts to zoom, show information, or list all shortcuts.
- The window no longer shows the app name or a dark title bar. The traffic-light buttons sit on the content, and the window uses the system dark frosted-glass background.
- On-screen wording is shorter and written for people using the app, not for diagnosing the system. Paths, network interface names, and raw error details are no longer shown.
- Icons use the official Kubai color variant. Icons on amber buttons stay dark and monochrome so they remain readable.
- ChillDINGothic is bundled in the app and works offline. This began in v0.1.1.
