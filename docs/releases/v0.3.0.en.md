# v0.3.0

- Opening a photo in Photos takes you to a full detail page. You can move to the previous or next photo, switch between fitting the image to the window and viewing it at display size, and read photographer information, including exposure, lens and crop, Image Control, focus, and a histogram. Press I to show or hide the information panel.
- When a DNG contains an embedded preview, the album grid shows a thumbnail. The detail page can also develop a preview from the RAW data, and it labels whether you are looking at a RAW develop or an embedded preview. The developed image is sized for the screen; it is not the original photograph at 100%.
- You can rate a photo from 1 to 5 stars, or mark it as picked or rejected, and you can filter the album by rating or by flag. Marks are stored next to the photograph and do not rewrite the original file.
- Saving a star or a flag now shows when the write is in progress or when it has been saved. Deleting a saved camera network asks for confirmation first. If a filter keeps the photo you are viewing even though it does not match, that photo is clearly labeled. When you are looking at a RAW develop or an embedded preview, the control is called Display size, so it is not confused with original photograph pixels.
- This release is for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. The app uses a personal signature and is not notarized by Apple. Original photographs are still only read, never changed. Connecting a camera and importing photos were already available; go by the connection state and photographer information you actually see with your own camera and files.
